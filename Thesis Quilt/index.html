Typographic Thesis Quilt!

This quilt was physical result on including the practices of both graphic design and fabric with handemade practices. The goal of producing this quilt was to bring together a space where I could research all skills I've taken in throughout my eduction in both design and printmaking, which resulted in a series of type design, machine sewing, and lots of digital layouts of this one quilt. The result concluded with over 1,000 fabric squares sewn together into the phrase Handemade with Love.
